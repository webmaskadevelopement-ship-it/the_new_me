About Us

We are a company dedicated to better health through simple, sustainable change. Our work is rooted in whole plant-based nutrition, practical lifestyle shifts, and the belief that daily movement is not optional, it is essential.

Our mission is to reach as many people as possible. The New Me book and our other products are created to empower you. You will notice the difference in your energy, your skin, your face, and your overall vitality. And once you do, going back will no longer feel like an option. We are here to help you eat well, look good, and most importantly, feel good.

Our Mission

Wellness is a return to natural vitality, and natural vitality leads to healthy longevity. At our company, we believe in taking informed choices. We aim to help people move away from an obsessive focus on weight loss, which often damages emotional and mental health, and instead towards sustainable habits and behavioural changes that last.

Our Vision

To invest in health and transform lives by making whole plant-based nutrition, practical lifestyle changes, and accessible workout guidance available to people of all ages.

Brand Story

About Gagan Dhawan

For years, Gagan lived the classic high-performance life: long hours, late nights, and constant stress. He built various successful businesses and niche-based products.

Until his body pushed back.

Facing burnout, fatigue, and lifestyle-related health issues, he decided to rebuild, not with shortcuts or trends, but through discipline, plant-based nutrition, and longevity science. That journey became The New Me, first as a book, then as a movement, and now as a complete wellness ecosystem.
